# Review checklist

The agent drafts. You decide what gets submitted. Work through this list before you paste the draft into your intake form.

- [ ] **Every `[INFERRED]` entry is checked.** Confirm each one with the source or the person who knows, then change the tag to `[SOURCE]` or rewrite the entry.
- [ ] **Every `[CONFLICT]` is resolved.** Pick the current version, note who confirmed it, and delete the other.
- [ ] **Blocker questions are sent.** Questions that block requirements work have gone to the people who can answer them, internal and external.
- [ ] **No personal data is copied in.** No names of the customers, patients, members, or other individuals the request is about, no account or record numbers, and no other personal data. Refer to them by role or record type.
- [ ] **Related intakes are opened.** You have read each linked prior intake and decided whether it changes this request or duplicates it.
- [ ] **The readiness verdict still holds.** After your edits, recount the sections that still need answers and update the Readiness line.
